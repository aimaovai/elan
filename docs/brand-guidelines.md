# Brand guidelines

ÉLAN is a fictional, contemporary fine-dining concept. Its voice is warm, precise, sensory, and understated. The primary line is “A seasonal expression of place, craft, and memory.” Avoid claims of awards, real-world affiliation, or operational status.

## Visual system

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Ivory | `#F5F1EA` | Editorial page backgrounds |
| Warm paper | `#EBE5DC` | Alternating sections |
| Charcoal | `#171715` | Primary text and footer |
| Soft charcoal | `#292925` | Supporting dark surfaces |
| Muted burgundy | `#653A42` | Restrained brand accent |
| Deep wine | `#44272F` | Dramatic feature sections |
| Champagne | `#B69A72` | Fine highlight and detail |
| Muted olive | `#757766` | Secondary natural accent |

Typography pairs a high-contrast editorial serif with a clean sans-serif. Use generous whitespace, asymmetrical image-led layouts, fine rules, short uppercase eyebrows, and restrained transitions. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

All restaurant location, chef, operating, and contact content is invented for a portfolio demonstration; no reservations or private dining inquiries are accepted.
